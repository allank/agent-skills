## Customer Value / JTBD

When I've just closed a sprint retro and the action items are scattered across
three tools, I want a single place to log and track them, so I can walk into
the next retro knowing what actually got done.

## Hypotheses

We believe faster sprint velocity will be achieved if engineering managers
attain a single source of truth for action items with a lightweight retro
tracker.

## Before / after — coaching for tightness

- **Before (wordy):** "We think that if we build some kind of tool that helps
  teams keep track of the various action items that come out of their sprint
  retrospectives in a more organized and centralized way, it will probably
  lead to better follow-through and maybe improve velocity over time."
- **After (tight):** "We believe faster sprint velocity will be achieved if
  engineering managers attain a single source of truth for action items with
  a lightweight retro tracker."
